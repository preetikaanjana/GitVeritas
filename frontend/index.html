Frequently Asked Questions

📊

How does the alignment score work?

The alignment score is the percentage of skills listed on your resume that we could verify through public contributions, languages, or notebook imports on your GitHub profile. Having some unverified skills is completely normal, but a higher score shows strong evidence of practical experience.

🔑

What if my commits are not showing up?

GitHub attributes commits to users based on the email address in the git config. If you used your student email locally but your personal email on GitHub, your commits might not count towards your profile. GitVeritas bypasses this limit for repos you own directly, so your personal projects still verify your skills!

📓

How are Jupyter Notebooks analyzed?

We recursively scan all directories in your repositories for `.ipynb` files. We parse the notebook's code cells line-by-line, detect imports of packages like `numpy`, `pandas`, or `scikit-learn`, and map them to your resume skills.

🛡️

Is my resume data stored?

No. We parse your resume temporarily in-memory to perform the consistency audit. Your data is never stored, sold, or shared with third parties.
